# Credex AI Spend Auditor

AI infrastructure cost optimization platform built for the Credex Web Development Assignment 2026.

---

##  Overview

Credex AI Spend Auditor helps startups and teams analyze their AI tooling costs and discover optimization opportunities.

The platform identifies:

- Overspending on AI subscriptions
- Unused or extra seats
- Expensive pricing plans
- API inefficiencies
- Enterprise optimization opportunities

Users can generate intelligent AI audit reports with estimated monthly and annual savings.
